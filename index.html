Prosper Loan Data Exploration

An exploratory data analysis of Prosper's loan dataset (113,937 loans and 81 columns) using Python in Jupyter Notebook. I examined how loan amounts and borrower interest rates relate to income range, employment status, and loan term, using histograms, count plots, box plots, and scatter plots to surface the patterns.

Key findings: Borrowers in higher income ranges generally received larger loans. 60-month loans tended to be larger than 36-month and 12-month loans. Larger loans were generally associated with lower interest rates, and loan amounts showed clear outliers.

Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

SQL Murder Mystery

A data investigation of a murder in SQL City. I started from the crime scene report, identified the two witnesses, joined the person, interview, and check-in tables to read their statements, and used the clues about a gym membership number and a license plate to narrow the field to two suspects. Checking their interview records led to a confession and the identification of the murderer, Jeremy Bowers.

Skills shown: SELECT, WHERE, AND, IN, LIKE, JOINs, date filtering, and following evidence across related tables.

Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard giving executives a clear view of Superstore sales from 2014 to 2017. It tracks total sales, profit, orders, and quantity sold, with sales trends by month, category, and region, top and bottom products, and Year, Region, and Category filters so users can explore the data themselves.

Key insights: The business generated $2.30M in sales and $286K in profit. Sales rose 242.69% between January and December 2014, the West region led with about $0.73M, and Technology was the top category at 36.4% of sales.

Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data visualization
